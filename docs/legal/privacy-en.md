## 1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information SCORECORE (the “App”) handles, how it is stored and synced, and what choices you have. By installing or using the App, you acknowledge this Policy.

## 2. Information we handle

As of the last updated date, the App:

- **Does not require account registration.** We do not collect account identifiers such as email, name, or phone number for sign-up.
- Stores **tournament, match, player names, scores, and preferences you enter** **locally on your device**.
- Does **not** include third-party advertising SDKs.
- Does **not** include third-party analytics / tracking SDKs.

The App does not upload personal information to our servers for advertising or analytics, and does not transmit data for those purposes.

## 3. Storage and sync

- **Local storage:** Match history and preferences are kept on the device(s) where you install the App (phone and/or watch).
- **Wear Data Layer:** Google Play services Wearable / Data Layer is used **solely to sync scoring state between your paired phone and watch**. It is not used for advertising, analytics, or marketing.
- **Network:** Network use is limited to companion sync and store-related system services (such as Google Play).

## 4. Sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information and do not share it with third parties through advertising or analytics SDKs. System features provided by the device maker, OS, or Google Play services (for example Wearable Data Layer, install/update) may be subject to those providers’ own policies.

## 5. Retention and deletion

Data is primarily retained on your devices. Uninstalling the App generally removes local data stored by the App on that device. Data remaining on a paired companion device (phone or watch) should be removed by uninstalling the App there or using in-app clear/delete options if available.

## 6. Children

The App is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 7. Permissions and system services

OS permissions and Google Play services may be used only as needed for core App features such as scoring and phone–watch sync.

## 8. Changes

We may update this Policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. We may provide reasonable notice of material changes. Continued use after changes constitutes acknowledgment of the revised Policy.
